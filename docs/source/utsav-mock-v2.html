02 — CALENDAR

A panchang that's right for where you live.

The daily reason to open it.

Most calendars you'll find online are computed for sunrise in India. Ours is computed for sunrise where you are, and for the tradition you follow — because Ekadashi genuinely does fall on different days depending on who you ask.

  • Today's tithi, paksha, masa and nakshatra
  • Fasting days with enough notice to prepare

03 — SEVA

Offer your time, and have it recognised.

Recorded, not rewarded.

Seva is given without expecting anything back, and we've built it that way — there are no points here, and nothing to redeem. What we do give you is a record: your hours, confirmed by a named person at the mandir, in a form an assessor or an employer will accept.

OUR PROMISE

We will never sell your data.

Where you worship and when you attend is among the most sensitive information there is about a person — it's treated as a special category under UK data protection law, and we treat it that way too.

We hold as little as we can, we never ask you your religion, and there is no arrangement under which any of it is sold, licensed or shared with advertisers or brokers. There isn't a price. This is the whole business, not a policy page.

Why your setting matters

You've chosen Amanta reckoning, where the month ends at the new moon. Under Purnimanta reckoning — common across North India — the same day sits in the following month, so the month name changes even though the tithi does not.

How it's calculated

Astronomy, then tradition, then a panditji.

Tithi is derived from the angular difference between the sun and the moon, reckoned at sunrise for your location. That part is arithmetic. Which observance falls on which tithi is not arithmetic — it's tradition, and it varies. So every calendar we publish is checked against a real one before it goes out.

WHAT IT COSTS

Nothing, and here's why.

Listings, bookings for free events and the seva roster cost nothing, and we're not planning to charge for them. Ticketing platforms already compete down to pennies a ticket and we're not going to win that fight, so we're not fighting it — if you sell tickets, keep using whatever you use and we'll link to it.

We'd rather be the thing every mandir uses than the thing forty mandirs pay for.

No contract, no lock-in

Export your data whenever you want. Ask us to remove your listing and it's gone the same day.

No committee training needed

If someone can use WhatsApp, they can post an event. There's nothing to learn.

We don't sell anything about your members

Not to advertisers, not to brokers, not to anyone. Ever.
