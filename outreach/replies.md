## What the replies tell us

- **WhatsApp is universal** (all five), then Facebook (4), Instagram (2),
  website and notice boards. Two also use **email lists**.
- **West Bromwich still prints a patrika**, in English and Gujarati, 3–4 weeks
  ahead, hand-delivered to about 350 households, then posted on Facebook and
  Instagram. That is exactly what Toran's poster queue is for: one
  forwarded patrika becomes listed events. Bilingual supports the Gujarati work.
- **Volunteers** come from existing volunteer pools, asked again on
  WhatsApp and at gatherings. Nobody described a sign-up system. The seva
  rota's "send to your WhatsApp group" fits how they already work.
- **Jalaram, Leicester is recruiting young volunteers** and has a
  young-generation trustee: a natural first pilot for the seva rota.
- Three offered a named contact or a call (West Bromwich, Southampton,
  Jalaram). Those are the best pilot candidates.
