1. **Calls (about 5 minutes each):** Leeds, Manor Park, Leicester (Shree
   Shakti) and Medway. Ask who handles events and volunteers, and for an

   update `tracker.csv`.
2. **Sivayogam:** call first to ask where the temple is now; if it has moved
   or closed, update or hide its page in `/ops/mandirs`.
3. **Gravesend and Colchester:** check the two possible matches. If the
   mandir in our list doesn't exist, hide it in `/ops/mandirs` so the
   directory stays accurate.

Phone numbers came from public registers. Check them before calling, as
registers can be out of date.
